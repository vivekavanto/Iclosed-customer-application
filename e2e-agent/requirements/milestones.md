# Status Overview milestones

Run each section with a client whose deal is that type.

## Purchase

### MS-P1 Purchase milestone list
Start: /dashboard
Auth: yes
- The Status Overview shows exactly these steps, in this order: Agreement received, Your information and ID, Title search, Mortgage instructions, Home insurance, Signing appointment, Closing funds, Purchase complete, Final report.
- There is no "File opened" or "Title transferred & keys released" step.

### MS-P2 Purchase steps follow their tasks
Start: /dashboard
Auth: yes
- "Agreement received" is ticked only if the agreement of purchase and sale task is completed.
- "Your information and ID" is ticked only if every personal information and identification task is completed.
- Exactly one step is marked as the current step, and it is the first step that is not ticked.
- The progress bar is roughly the share of ticked steps out of 9.

## Sale

### MS-S1 Sale milestone list
Start: /dashboard
Auth: yes
- The Status Overview shows exactly these steps, in this order: Agreement received, Your information and ID, Mortgage payout statement, Property tax and rentals, Signing appointment, Sale complete, Proceeds sent to you, Final report.
- There is no "File opened", "Title search" or "Title transferred & keys released" step.

### MS-S2 Property tax and rentals needs both tasks
Start: /dashboard
Auth: yes
- "Property tax and rentals" is ticked only when both the property tax task and the rental equipment task are completed. If either is still open in the task list, the step is not ticked.
- Exactly one step is marked as the current step, and it is the first step that is not ticked.
